From site review to go-live

Your rollout plan defines responsibilities, dates and what the team will test.

  1. 01

    Review your store

    Identify checkout lanes, current devices, payment workflows and opening dates.

  2. 02

    Prepare the catalog

    Review product identifiers, pricing, variants and the inventory data available for import.

  3. 03

    Install and configure

    Set up the equipment, store settings and staff roles defined in the project scope.

  4. 04

    Test real transactions

    Walk through sales, returns, discounts, scanning, payment and end-of-day reporting.

  5. 05

    Train and switch over

    Give staff time to practice and agree on the changeover, fallback plan and handoff.
